## Runtime-executed vs unit-tested

* Runtime-executed in tests: every importer on generated files (CP1250, ISO-8859-2, UTF-8 with and
  without BOM, CP1252; decimal comma/point; `-1,00`, `1,00-`, `(1.00)`, U+2212; debit/credit columns;
  Af/Bij indicator; undecidable day/month order; duplicated rows; transit journeys with midnight
  rollover; tap pairing; rides across the 2024-03-31 DST change; CAMT.053 v2 and v8 with `Dt` and
  `DtTm`, PDNG and reversal entries, a balance mismatch; MT940 with envelope, three statements,
  year-boundary and ambiguous entry dates, RD reversal, an unparsable `:61:`; Apple Health
  `export.xml` and zip with internal DTD, mixed offsets, DST-spanning records, two devices'
  overlapping nights; Google Fit sessions and metrics; Samsung sleep/exercise/steps/unmapped tables;
  Fitbit sleep and exercise), opt-in refusals, re-import idempotence, the CLI in a subprocess, the
  HTTP routes (TestClient), the import plan end to end (catalog scan → plan → decisions → execution →
  re-run), and the time budget over imported sessions and rides.
* Bounded streaming of Apple Health measured with `tracemalloc` (2 000 vs 8 000 extra records: peak
  not doubled); a manual run of 80 000 records showed a flat ~16 MB peak.
* Not run: real bank, transit, ride or health exports (none available); XLS (BIFF8) input (reader
  shared with the scanner, not exercised here); a real Samsung or Fitbit export layout; any network
  call (none exists).

## Boundaries

Profiles, detector markers and health mappings are examples from public observations, not
verified layouts. Spreadsheet numeric cells arrive as floats from the reader and are flagged.
Health data are special-category: imported only with `enable_health` per run, processed locally,
exported in the private metadata package like other observations.
